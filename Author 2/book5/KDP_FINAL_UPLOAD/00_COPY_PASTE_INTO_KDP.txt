Roar Wonders is a wild animal coloring book for kids ages 4-8, with 50 big, bold animals from around the world to color, and a real animal fact on every page.

Meet a roaring lion on the savanna, a sleepy sloth in the rainforest and a giant panda munching bamboo. Then travel on to Asia, North America, the deserts and the poles, and finish in Australia with a kangaroo and a koala. Every design has thick outlines and big open spaces, so young explorers can finish each page and feel proud of it.

Inside this animal coloring book:

• 50 Original Designs: lions, elephants, giraffes, tigers, pandas, sloths, toucans, bears, penguins and many more.
• A Trip Around the World: from the African savanna and the Amazon rainforest to snowy mountains, hot deserts and icy poles.
• Big & Bold Pages: Extra-thick outlines and large open spaces, great for crayons, markers and little hands.
• Fun Facts on Every Page: Learn real animal secrets as you color, like why a camel has a hump and how far a lion's roar can travel.
• Single-Sided Pages: Every design has a blank back, so markers don't bleed through and finished pictures are ready to display.
• Bonus Pages: A Color Test Palette, a checklist to check off all 50 animals, and a Wild Explorer Artist Award.
• Large 8.5 x 11 inch Format: Crisp, high-resolution prints.
• A Great Gift: For birthdays, Christmas stockings, classrooms and kids who love animals.

Grab your colors, little explorers, and roar into the wild!
