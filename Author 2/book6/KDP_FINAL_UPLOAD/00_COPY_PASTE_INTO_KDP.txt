Description (Copy and paste directly into the KDP text box):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Stomp Wonders is a dinosaur coloring book for kids ages 4-8, with 50 big, bold dinosaurs and prehistoric reptiles to color, and a real dinosaur fact on every page.

Meet a T. rex lifting a log with its tiny arms, a Triceratops and her baby in a fern meadow, and a Brachiosaurus nibbling the treetops. Then soar with a Pteranodon, swim with a Plesiosaurus, visit an Aussie dinosaur corner and finish with a fossil dig. Every design has thick outlines and big open spaces, so young paleontologists can finish each page and feel proud of it.

Inside this dinosaur coloring book:

• 50 Original Designs: T. rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Velociraptor, Spinosaurus, Ankylosaurus and many more.
• Real Dinosaur Facts on Every Page: Learn what a thagomizer is, which dinosaur had 15 horns and why a chicken is a cousin of T. rex.
• Aussie Dinosaurs: Meet Muttaburrasaurus, Minmi, Australovenator (nicknamed Banjo) and the giant Australotitan.
• Flying and Swimming Reptiles: Pteranodon, Quetzalcoatlus, Plesiosaurus and Ichthyosaurus, with the facts that tell them apart from dinosaurs.
• Big & Bold Pages: Extra-thick outlines and large open spaces, great for crayons, markers and little hands.
• Single-Sided Pages: Every design has a blank back, so markers don't bleed through and finished pictures are ready to display.
• Bonus Pages: A Color Test Palette, a checklist to check off all 50 pages, and a Dino Explorer Artist Award.
• Large 8.5 x 11 inch Format: Crisp, high-resolution prints.
• A Great Gift: For birthdays, Christmas stockings, classrooms and kids who love dinosaurs.

Grab your colors, little explorers, and stomp into the past!
