# Author 2 Book 6: "Stomp Wonders": Page-by-Page Generation Instructions (50 pages)

> House: Wattle Wonders Publishing / pen Flora Watt. Lane A. Bold & easy thick-line interiors,
> 10-14 elements per scene, art fills a bigger area (fit 2400x2700), fun fact on every page,
> captions in Fredoka. Engine: batch_generate_a2.py (copied from book5, rule 15). OpenArt CLI / nano-banana-2 for pages.
> Title: "Stomp Wonders" (validation: publishing\13-validation-stomp-wonders.md).
> Mix: famous five first, then giants, meat-eaters, horns and frills, crests and nests, feathered
> dinosaurs, before the dinosaurs, sky and sea reptiles (labelled "not a dinosaur" in the facts),
> an Aussie dinosaurs corner (5), and discoveries (egg, footprints, fossil dig, birds).
> Every dinosaur is a friendly character with a happy, closed-mouth smile. Nothing scary: no hunting,
> no bared teeth, no fighting. Scene plants are prehistoric: ferns, cycads, palm-like trees, conifers,
> horsetails. No gum trees in the Aussie corner (they came later). No color words in prompts.

## Universal prompt suffix (LOCKED: append to EVERY scene prompt)

```
Bold and easy coloring book page. A rich, detailed but simple prehistoric scene with 10 to 14 easy elements arranged to fill the whole page around the main dinosaur (ferns, prehistoric plants, rocks and things that belong in its world, plus a few sparkles), all part of one connected scene, never a border of separate stickers or icons around the edge, each element drawn as one big simple closed shape. The composition fills almost the entire page. Whole scene floats with a plain white margin on every side; no element touches the image edges. Absolutely no rectangular frame or box line anywhere; do not draw a border. Very thick, clean, smooth black outlines (heavy, marker-friendly), pure black on plain white only. Only simple closed shapes with large open areas to color. The main dinosaur stays the largest thing on the page, portrait composition. Cute, friendly, happy faces with large simple eyes and closed-mouth smiles, no sharp teeth showing. No shading, no grey, no color fill, no texture, no crosshatching, no scale patterns. No words, letters, numbers, logos or symbols anywhere in the image. Nothing in the bottom 6 percent of the page.
```

Regeneration wording for edge or frame flags (F2/F3), added in front of the suffix on rerolls only:

```
The whole scene is one floating vignette in the middle of the page, like an island, with a wide plain white margin on all four sides; the ground ends in a soft rounded edge well inside the page and nothing reaches the edges.
```
