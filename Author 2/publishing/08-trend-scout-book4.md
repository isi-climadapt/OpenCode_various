Sample (rule 8): **104 books**: 72 organic rows from the first three best-seller-sorted pages of "coloring book for kids" (Books) on amazon.com, plus 32 from the first two best-seller-sorted pages of "colouring book for kids" on amazon.com.au. Sponsored rows excluded. ## Ranked shortlist

1. **THINGS THAT GO with fun facts** ⭐ RECOMMENDED. 50 vehicles across land, sea, air and space (fire truck, digger, tractor, school bus, road train, steam train, sailing ship, submarine, hot air balloon, helicopter, rocket, Mars rover cameo) with one true fact each. Why: highest demand of any theme the brand can enter (Truck 2.2K in the top 15, five vehicle titles in the top 72, 2.2K and 602 on .com.au); the facts competitors are either realistic-art (Coolest Trucks, 132) or single-type toddler books (Little Rippers); fits the Wattle Wonders boy-coded edu lane and the Christmas gift window. Australian twist available (road trains, Flying Doctor plane, Melbourne tram, ute, surf rescue boat) without narrowing the US appeal. Risk: zero-review supply is arriving monthly and skews toddler, so the cover must sell "ages 4-8, big book, real facts" (age band locked in 09 under rule 16) at thumbnail size.
2. **WEATHER AND PLANET EARTH with fun facts.** Open bold-easy facts lane and easy to draw (volcanoes, rainbows, storms, caves, waterfalls). Risk: modest demand, lower ceiling. Good Book 5.
3. Science lab. Later, once the house has reviews.
4. Sports. Licensing and US-sports voice risk.
