## 3. Author-brand naming rules (from the winners)

- **Two-word novel pairing** wins for personality brands: "Coco Wyo", "Southern Lotus" — memorable, non-generic, no suffix = personality
- Suffix semantics: **"Publishing" = institution/trust** (FairyWren, AU-native speller); **"Books" = volume farm** (Blue Jewel — also runs the dual-credit "BJB Publishing" trick); pen names carry NO suffix
- **Native-fauna noun = instant geo-identity** (FairyWren + AU spelling)
- Real-name artist = premium "gift-book" signal (F4)

## 4. What Author 2 must AVOID

1. Literal "Cozy/Cutie/Cutting Cozy" brand words — Coco Wyo owns the register AND Author 1 lives next door
2. "Bold and Easy" as a brand (only as title descriptor where useful) — saturated to commodity
3. White-clipart volume-farm look — unwinnable vs 85.8K review moats, damages pricing
4. Anything cream/pastel kawaii-cover shaped — cannibalises Author 1 in the same SERPs
5. Head-on cloning (fantasy-dwelling triplet clones at 4.2★ show the floor)

## 5. THREE candidate lanes for Author 2 (decision-ready)

### Lane A — "Premium Educational House" ⭐ RECOMMENDED
- **Look:** bright primary/desaturated-bold palette (NOT cream), dense designed scene covers or single bold-icon anchor, "publishing house" wordmark, publisher-declared age bands
- **Voice:** energetic, "Big Book of / The Complete / Wonders of" educational register, gender-split-friendly topics
- **Audience:** kids 3–8 + gift/edu buyers (deepest purchase pool)
- **Evidence:** FairyWren proves the trust voice (1.9K) but at F3-grade covers → pairing their voice with F4-grade cover craft = unoccupied; boy-coded openings (soccer/robots/space) nearly empty with designed covers; non-animal premium themes open
- **Fit with our machine:** same 108pp/fact/pipeline economics, different art style + brand colours + typography; does NOT collide with Author 1

### Lane B — Pipe-Syntax Parenting-Trust Utility (toddlers 1–3)
- **Look:** clean white/graphite flat design, no cute iconography, uniform plate style, benefit-stacked pipe subtitles
- **Evidence:** Jonas Jansen ~750 rev at flat $15.95 premium; milestone-gifting niche; low competition
- **Risk:** narrowest pool, toddler covers tolerate less art style range

### Lane C — Adult Premium "Calm Detail" (18–50)
- **Look:** muted jewel/ink covers, dark or high-contrast line art, scene-dense architectural/fantasy dwellings
- **Evidence:** $35 price screen proven (Still & Slow), Fairyland 2.1K demand; wording axis = "Mindful/Calm/Detailed" (NEVER cozy/hygge)
- **Risk:** different production pipeline (denser line art = more credits/page), different buyer psychology
