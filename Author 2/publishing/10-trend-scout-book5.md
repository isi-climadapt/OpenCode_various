## Ranked shortlist

1. **WILD ANIMALS OF THE WORLD with fun facts** ⭐ RECOMMENDED. 50 animals from six habitats (savanna, rainforest, forest, mountains, desert, polar and wetlands), one true fact each: lion, elephant, giraffe, zebra, hippo, cheetah, gorilla, sloth, toucan, jaguar, red panda, giant panda, tiger, moose, bear, beaver, wolf, owl, camel, fennec fox, meerkat, snow leopard, yak, penguin, polar bear and more. Why: animals are the largest non-licensed kids theme in the sample (Big Book of Colouring: Animals at #3); buyers type "animal coloring book with facts"; the facts leaders are only 400 and 224 reviews with dated covers; it fits the brand's "real facts woven into the fun" promise and broadens the series beyond boy-coded topics for gift buyers. Australian twist: an "Aussie corner" of 3 to 4 animals not in Author 1's set (platypus, echidna, cassowary, Tasmanian devil) is possible but optional, to keep clear of Aussie Cuties. Risk: overlap with Author 1's cute-animal books, so the art stays Wattle Wonders (bold scenes, habitat, fact on every page), sea animals stay out (Ocean Friends), and polar animals are limited to two or three (Snowy Cuties).
2. **DINOSAURS with fun facts.** Highest raw demand, a natural Wattle Wonders topic (the bio already names dinosaurs), and boy-coded. Risk: the facts lane is owned at 2K and 1.1K and new launches arrive weekly at 0 reviews. A better book once the series has reviews to lift it.
3. **WEATHER AND PLANET EARTH with fun facts.** Open lane, easy to draw (volcanoes, rainbows, storms, caves, waterfalls), but modest demand.
4. Science lab. Thin demand for ages 4-8 and a 2026 "little scientist" flood. Not recommended now.
