# Stage 1 — Trend Scout Report: Author 2 Book 2 (Flora Watt / Wattle Wonders Publishing)

> Data date: Oct 17 2026. Sample (pipeline rule 8): **100 books** — top 50 organic results by Amazon's best-seller sort for "colouring book for kids" on amazon.com.au + top 50 for "coloring book for kids" on amazon.com (sponsored rows excluded). Then theme-depth SERPs (relevance sort, page 1 organic) for robots, bugs and trucks on both markets. Review counts are SERP display figures. Lane A: Premium Educational House, bright covers, kids 3–8, 108pp/50 designs + fun facts.

---

## What the 100 best-sellers say

- **Licensed characters dominate .au** (Bluey, Disney, Barbie, Marvel, Wiggles, Pokémon ≈ 25 of 50) — unassailable, ignore.
- **Coco Wyo "Cozy" line** holds ~10 of the 100 slots across both markets (Cozy Cuties 5.6K) — Author 1's lane, not Author 2's.
- **Toddler first-words / dot-marker** books (Creative Toddler 8.8–9.1K, Simple & Big 7.9K) — different age band.
- **Vehicles are the strongest non-licensed kids theme**: Truck Coloring Book 2.2K, Things That Go 680, Monster Truck 223, plus licensed Hot Wheels / John Deere.
- **Facts + colouring sells at best-seller level**: *Bug Book for Kids: Coloring Fun and Awesome Facts* ("A Did You Know? Coloring Book") 1.2K is in the .com top 50; *Flags of the World* sticker-colouring 1.9K in the .au top 50. This is Author 2's exact format (facts on every page).
- **No space and no robot title in either top 50.** Space Wonders' theme sells below this tier; robots likewise.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **BUGS & MINIBEASTS with fun facts** ⭐ RECOMMENDED — the only candidate with facts-colouring demand in the top-100 sample; bold-easy versions are all zero-review; fits Flora Watt's canon ("tiny animals with big jobs"); broad appeal (boys + girls); Aussie angle available (Christmas beetle, Ulysses butterfly, bull ant, huntsman, glow-worms) as a differentiator. Working title in the house line: **"Bug Wonders"** (collision check in Stage 2).
2. **ROBOTS** — keeps the Space → Robots → Science series plan; thinnest competition, but weakest demand proof. Strong Book 3.
3. **TRUCKS & MACHINES** — strongest demand, wrong moment (launch flood). Revisit ~April 2027.
