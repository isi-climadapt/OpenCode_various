## Ranked shortlist

1. **DINOSAURS with fun facts** ⭐ RECOMMENDED. 50 dinosaurs and prehistoric animals, one true fact each: T. rex, Triceratops, Stegosaurus, Brachiosaurus, Velociraptor, Ankylosaurus, Spinosaurus, Parasaurolophus, Pachycephalosaurus, Diplodocus, plus flying and swimming reptiles, a fossil dig and a museum page, and an **Aussie dinosaurs corner** (Muttaburrasaurus, Australovenator, Minmi, Leaellynasaura, Diamantinasaurus) as the Wattle Wonders home-country nod. Why: the deepest kids coloring demand on Amazon.com, the only theme Amazon autocompletes to a kids age band, a facts lane whose leaders are six-plus years old, and a topic the Flora Watt bio already names. Fresh 2026 launches are almost all plain coloring, activity or story books. Risk: high launch volume (about two a day), so the cover must win at thumbnail size and the series (five books by then) should cross-promote. Clash check (Oct 24): the Author 1 Book 6 session confirms it left dinosaurs off on purpose; its pick is sweet treats with cute animals (backup: capybara and friends). Neither overlaps.
2. **AROUND THE WORLD: landmarks, flags and kids of the world.** Mid demand, open designed-cover lane. Risk: repeats Book 5's around-the-world format and landmarks need accurate architecture.
3. **WEATHER AND PLANET EARTH with fun facts.** Open lane, easy to draw (volcanoes, rainbows, storms, caves, waterfalls), modest demand.
4. Jobs and community helpers. Autocomplete signal but no coloring book has proven it. Pass.
