## 5. Content plan (50 designs)

Mix backyard favourites (ladybird, honeybee, butterfly, ant, dragonfly, snail, worm, spider, caterpillar, grasshopper, firefly…) with Australian stars as the differentiator (Christmas beetle, Ulysses butterfly, bull ant, rainbow stag beetle, glow-worms of the caves, giant burrowing cockroach, stick insect, emperor gum moth caterpillar, cicada "green grocer", leaf-cutter bee…). One kid-friendly fact per page, AU spelling. Spiders and other non-insects allowed (minibeasts), each fact accurate.

## 6. Differentiation checklist (house rules)

2. 108pp / 50 single-sided designs + fact on every page (nobody holds the full combo)
3. Hygiene: perfect spelling, AU "Colouring" on .au, no pipe-spam
4. A+ content from day 1 (Stage 7)
5. A$16.99 launch / floor A$13.99; US $9.99; UK £7.99
