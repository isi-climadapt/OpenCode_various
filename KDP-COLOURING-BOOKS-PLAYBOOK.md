## 1. The Business Model & Reality Check

### What KDP actually is (Sean Dollwet, $2.2M+ royalties, 10+ yrs)
- KDP is **~80% hands-off**: Amazon prints and ships; you handle only 3 jobs:
  1. **Research profitable topics**
  2. **Create the book**
  3. **Marketing**
- No inventory, no customer service, no shipping — same as dropshipping's appeal but with better margins.

### Myths debunked (from the video)
| Myth | Truth |
|---|---|
| "You'll make $10K/month fast" | Requires business + marketing skills. Low-effort AI-spam books earn "a couple bucks." |
| "Amazon does everything for you" | ~80% hands-off. You still own the 3 jobs above. |
| "Publish 1,000 blank journals and get rich" | A pen name with **141 low-quality books ≈ $0**. One good journal did $2,000/mo — quality > volume. **Few quality books beat pennies from many.** |
| "Follow your passion" | **Start with demand before you create** ("write to market"). Formula + a unique touch = original enough. |
| "KDP is saturated" | Most books are done badly. Do the basics and you're ahead of 99%. KDP monthly author payouts grew from ~$19M to ~$64-69M over the last decade. |

### The makeup of a $10K/mo business (Sean's live examples)
- Page-one books already selling = proof of demand. Examples read via BookBeam: 55 reviews → **$200/day**; other books $49/day, $1,000/day, $610/day.
- **Student case studies** (not typical): 18-year-old 2 months in → $6K/mo revenue; one month → $1K/mo profit with no ads.

### Reality data (Self Publishing Empire, 6.5-yr transparency video)
- **201 books over 6.5 years = $146,663 royalties** (~$733/book lifetime, ~$525 net after ads)
- $44,604 ad spend → $193K sales @ 23.13% ACoS; $0.29 avg CPC
- **Expect only 1-in-3 to 1-in-8 books to become a real success**
- Biggest month: Dec 2025 → $15,573 (Q4 gifting spike is real — build catalog *ahead* of Q4)
- **"Organic sales are dead in 2026 without ads"** (exception: hungry niches)

### The 2025/26 ideological map
- **Sean Dollwet**: scale on Amazon, premium pricing, chase demand, translations, ads → rank not profit.
- **Godlywood Girl (Stephanie)**: Amazon takes too much (book at $6.99 nets 25–35¢ sometimes); sell **direct** via own website + email list; use Amazon only as funnel front-end. (Dissenting but useful for list-building.)
- **Ken Fornari**: most operational 12-phase framework (see §6, §7).
- **Joshua Mayo**: AI coloring book micro-course (see §4).

## 2. Niche Research & Validation

### Golden rule: what sells well looks like this
- **"Winning book"** = ≤250 reviews AND BSR ≤30,000 (Ken Fornari's validation metric)
- **"Dead book"** = 30+ reviews AND BSR ≥150,000
- Avoid topics where every page-one book has 500+ reviews
- Target categories where **top-20 BSR sits 10K–40K** (grabs the bestseller orange badge)

### Frameworks (pick one, use it every time)

**5-point framework (BookIllustrationAI 2026):**
1. Result count **200–2,000 = sweet spot**; >5,000 saturated; <200 verify demand first
2. BSR: **<50K = multiple copies/day; 50–100K = 1–3/day; >200K = occasional**. Need ≥3 of top-10 under 100K. All top-10 >300K = searchers but no buyers
3. Competition quality: read top covers/descriptions/top-3 review depth
4. **Price viability: $9.99 is the new floor** (post-June-2025 royalty structure; <$9.99 = 50% royalty tier). Niche that only sustains $5.99 ≈ $0.88/sale → skip
5. Community signal: subreddit 5K+, FB groups, Pinterest saves sustained
- Time: 15–20 min per niche → run 5–10 ideas → 1–2 viable

**7-point checklist (KDPEasy):** pass ≥5/7 → launch
- 300+ results minimum (200–2,000 sweet spot) ✅ top-10 cover quality beatable ✅ ≥3 of top-10 BSR<100K ✅ 2–3 books with 50+ reviews ✅ top sellers $8.99+ ✅ community 5K+ ✅ Google Trends stable/growing ✅

**Scored model (InkChamps, 500 books analyzed May 2026):** Demand 30% + Competition gap 25% + Production fit 20% + Price fit 15% ($9.99+ supportable) + IP/policy risk 10%. Bands: 80–100 build a test book; 65–79 narrow the angle; 50–64 only with clearly better execution; <50 skip.

### The #1 secret weapon: read 1-star reviews
Incumbents' 1-star reviews = **your book spec checklist**. Common gaps:
- "Designs too small" / "lines too thin for markers"
- "Not enough pages" / "not actually [theme]"
- Marker bleed-through → print **single-sided pages** (non-negotiable)

## 3. Royalty Math & Pricing

### The formula (paperback, amazon.com)
**royalty = (rate × list price) − print cost**
- Rate: **60% if list ≥ $9.99**; **50% if $9.98 or lower** (June 2025 change)
- Expanded Distribution: 40% — NOT available to low-content books
- **$9.99 is the strategic floor.** $8.99 pays $1.66; $9.99 pays $3.15 on large-trim B&W. In the 500-book sample, **$8.99 was the most common price among sub-100K-BSR books (34%) — a $1 mistake that nearly halves royalties.**

### Print costs (amazon.com paperback)
| Spec | Cost |
|---|---|
| B&W white/cream, large trim (8.5×11), **≤110 pages** | **$2.84 flat** |
| B&W >110 pages, large trim | $1.00 + $0.017/page |
| B&W ≤110 pages, regular trim (6×9) | $2.30 flat |
| Standard color, large trim | $1.00 + $0.0402/page |
| Premium color, 24–40 pages large | $4.20 flat |
| Premium color >42 pages, large | $1.00 + $0.080/page |
| Groundwood (B&W only, cheapest) | ~5% less than white; ≤112pp fixed cost lower |

### Worked examples — 8.5×11
| Book | Print cost | Price | Royalty |
|---|---|---|---|
| 100pp B&W coloring book | $2.84 | $9.99 | **$3.15** |
| 100pp B&W | $2.84 | $12.99 | **$4.95** |
| 100pp standard color | $5.02 | $9.99 | $0.97 ❌ |
| 100pp standard color | $5.02 | $12.99 | $2.77 ✅ |
| 100pp premium color | $9.00 | $15.99 | $0.59 ❌ (min $15.00 to break even) |
| 120pp premium color | $10.60 | $24.99 | $4.39 ✅ (grayscale territory) |

**Conclusion: coloring books print B&W (line art). Color only pays ≥$12.99 (standard) / ≥$15–17 (premium).** Anchor at the top of what your niche supports — never price into $5.99–$6.99 niches.

### Unit economics target (portfolio math)
100-page B&W at $9.99 = **$3.15/sale net**. A realistic top-100 category coloring book: ~360 units/mo × $2.64 ≈ $950/mo (top of category).
**Portfolio model:** 100 titles × 1.5 sales/mo × $2.50 margin ≈ **$375/mo baseline** — with 1-in-5 winners at BSR <20K driving most of it.

## 4. Production: Print Specs & Tools

### KDP official print spec cheat-sheet (8.5×11 large trim)
- **Interior**: print-ready PDF, embed fonts, 300 DPI images, flattened, CMYK, ≤40 MB recommended
- **Bleed**: if ONE page bleeds, whole file must bleed → make page size **8.625" × 11.25"** (trim + 0.125" sides/top/bottom; NOTHING added at spine)
- **Margins**: outside ≥0.25" no-bleed / **0.375" with bleed**; inside gutter by page count: 24–150pp = 0.375", 151–300pp = 0.5"
- **Page count**: B&W min 24; standard color min 72; single-sided interiors are the sanity default (blank backs = no marker bleed)
- **Cover**: one PDF = back + spine + front (+0.125" bleed each side). Height = 11.25".
  - **Spine width B&W = page count × 0.002252"** → 100 pages = 0.2252" → whole cover = **17.4752" × 11.25"**
  - Spine text only ≥79 pages; barcode box 2" × 1.2" lower-right back (leave empty)
  - Free official calculator: https://kdp.amazon.com/cover-calculator
- Low-content books may publish **without a free ISBN**

### Paper/ink choices that matter for coloring books
- **B&W on white paper** = default for line art on Amazon
- **cream** = warmer look, slightly wider spine
- **groundwood** = cheapest (~5% less), but darker/grainier print — risky for fine line art
- Standard color on 8.5×11 kills margin below $12.99 → stay B&W unless the niche is premium grayscale

### Recommended production stack (2026)
| Job | Tool | Cost |
|---|---|---|
| Niche/keyword research | Book Bolt (Newbie $9.99/mo, Pro $19.99/mo anual) or BookBeam | ~$10–20/mo |
| Interior + cover layout | Canva (page size 8.625×11.25 for bleed) | free tier OK |
| Vectorizing AI line art | Inkscape Trace Bitmap (Illustrator $22.99/mo alternative) | free |
| AI line art generation | Ideas: Midjourney / Ideogram / Recraft (vector-native SVG; paid plan required for commercial use) / local Stable Diffusion + ControlNet | free–$30/mo |
| Upscaling to 300 DPI | Upscayl (free) / Topaz Gigapixel | free–$ |
| Puzzle/word search | Book Bolt Pro puzzle engines | included |
| Assets with KDP resale rights | Creative Fabrica All Access ($29/mo, ~$19.75/mo annual) — POD license included; CFPOTD add-on ~$19.99/mo | $9–29/mo |
| Review copies | Booksprout / BookBounty | varies |

### The AI coloring book workflow (Joshua Mayo, 223K views)
1. **Niche down via Amazon autocomplete** ("coloring book for toddlers / anxiety / girls 8–12"), validate with review counts + units, mimic winning covers
2. Generate **~75 pages in ChatGPT**: ask the AI to write per-page image prompts FIRST, then generate; retry bad gens; **no copyrighted characters**
3. Generate front/back covers too (strip any fake barcode)
4. Assemble 8.5×11 in Canva → export PDF-print (flattened), covers PNG @2x, merge with KDP cover-calculator template
5. Upload: free KDP ISBN, B&W interior, no bleed, matte cover, **declare AI content**, price $9.99–10.99 ≈ **$4.29 royalty/sale**

### AI content rule (as of 2026)
KDP asks **one question per book**: is any text / image / translation AI-generated? Answer honestly — disclosure is stored internally (not shown publicly), lies = contract breach (takedown or account closure). AI-assisted (human-edited + own prompt-craft) vs AI-generated: AI-assisted does NOT require disclosure. Cover art counts.

**Anti-slop quality bar:** clean open lines, no accidental fills/sketchiness, no gray shading (prints muddy, flagged as #1 AI tell), mandala symmetry must be perfect. "Bold & easy" won partly because it is simple-at-thumbnail and immune to fake detail density.

## 5. Titles, Keywords & Covers

### Title formula (Ken Fornari / Sean consensus)
- **Main title = exact keyword**: "Chair Yoga for Weight Loss" — be literal, keyword-rich, not artsy ("Beautiful Cats" never outranks "Cat Coloring Book for Adults Stress Relief")
- **Subtitle = audience + benefit + bonus**: "50+ large-print designs for seniors and beginners | includes 20 easy practice pages"
- Coloring variant: **Profession+relief / Breed+mood / Audience×Theme×Format** stacking
- Kids books: buyer ≠ user — sell to the gift-giving parent/grandparent (state age range IN the title)

### The 7 backend keyword slots (no repeats of title words, zero subjective claims like "best", no brands)
Cozy example set:
`cozy coloring book for adults / bold easy coloring pages / relaxing cute home scenes / cottagecore coloring pages / simple adult coloring book / stress relief coloring pages / cozy gifts for book lovers`

Kids example set:
`coloring book for kids ages 4 8 / dinosaur coloring pages / easy coloring pages for boys girls / preschool kindergarten activity book / simple animal coloring pages / birthday gift for kids / toddler learning coloring book`

### Cover = 90% of the selling
- Show **finished colored artwork next to line art** (the single highest-value cover change of 2026)
- White/off-white background + one focal image; title readable **at thumbnail size** (the click is won in search results at ~150px)
- Hand-lettered/calligraphic title; textured paper-grain (botanical/vintage) / dark gradient (mandalas) / foil-look (dark fantasy)
- Avoid busy full-bleed backgrounds; avoid AI-looking tell-tale shading
- Outsource if needed: Fiverr $20–60; 100Covers packages from $100 (claims to have tracked 20,000+ covers' conversion)
- Bad-listing anatomy (real example quoted in the video): broad title, no subtitle, obvious template cover, **$25 price (2–3× market), zero reviews** → doesn't sell regardless of interior quality

### A+ Content (free, do this every book)
- 5–7 modules: cover banner / interior flip grid (970×600px) / "coloring tip" image / use-case collage
- **No invented customer quotes** (only quotes from well-known publications, max 4)
- Critical for low-content-adjacent books: no Look Inside without ISBN
- Goes live ~8 business days

## 6. Marketing & Amazon Ads

### When to run ads
**Gate: royalty ≥ $3 AND validated keyword demand.** Typical journal $6.99 ≈ $2 royalty cannot survive CPC >$0.30. Rule of thumb: **royalty ≥ 5× target CPC** before switching ads on.

### Campaign structure (BookBolt + Ken Fornari doctrine)
1. **Set an END DATE manually** — #1 disaster: eternal campaigns leaking money. Budget **$5/day** while learning.
2. **Start with Automatic** campaign 2–3 weeks → pull Search Term report → **promote winners to Manual** (exact/phrase).
3. Bids: start 20–30¢ CPC (cheap data) or suggested +10¢ for the "monopoly" play; **Dynamic bids — down only**; placement modifiers 0% initially.
4. **Negative keywords weekly**: "free", "printable", "online", generic terms converting at zero.
5. **Product targeting**: put your book on competitors' detail pages + complementary products (e.g., a keto tracker advertisement on keto cookbooks).
6. **Monopoly method**: 2–3 books of yours on the same keyword → Sponsored #1 + #2 → organic #1. You own the category page.
7. Weekly report review; biweekly prune. Trust KDP royalties for P&L (Ad reports ≠ KDP reports — 14-day attribution).

### The ads grand strategy (Ken Fornari)
- **Ads' goal = rank page-one for the main keyword, NOT direct profit.** "Unprofitable campaigns can still mean thousands in profit" (read-through + organic rank).
- Launch month #1: it's normal to LOSE money (case: −$230 in month 1 → $4,500 profit months; book stabilized at $2–4K/mo).
- Target ACoS: break-even = royalty ÷ price (e.g., $5.67/$14.99 ≈ 38% break-even; **aim ~20%**; overall TACOS <25%).
- Run ads at ~20 reviews, target crossing to ~100.

### Free traffic (the 2026 lever)
- **Pinterest = highest-ROI free channel**: business account, 10–15 boards × 20+ pins each; multi-variant Canva pins per book; rewrite pin descriptions with your listing keywords; repin competitor covers to keyword boards; 3–5 pins/week cadence analytically doubled-down.
- **TikTok/Reels/Shorts: faceless interior flip-through videos = highest-converting format.** One viral flip-through (guided-journal) took a BSR from 200,000 → 5,000 in 48h; guided-journal channel hit 70M views. Publish one Short per title, cross-post to YT Shorts + IG Reels.
- **Micro-influencers**: pay 10–20 TikTokers (5K–50K followers) with free copies for flip-through videos.
- **Email funnel (review-safe)**: free time-limited PDF sample → MailerLite capture → auto-sequence: Amazon link + your other books in the same series/pen name. (Godlywood's direct-sell crowd builds entire businesses on this; use it to drive Amazon velocity *and* build the list.)
- Amazon's algorithm rewards external traffic → sends it back to rank higher.

## 7. Reviews, Series & Scaling

### Reviews (only safe routes, per Amazon policy)
- **Free/ARC copies ARE allowed** — as long as you do NOT require a review in exchange or influence opinion
- Amazon Vine is invitation-based; reviewers must have $50 spend in last 12 months to leave reviews at all
- **NEVER**: pay-for-review packages, QR-code-in-the-book review asks, social media broker deals → review pulling + account risk (fatal)
- Book Bounty / Booksprout (10–20 reviews/week cadence is the expert norm)

### Series strategy
- **Coloring books are NOT low-content by Amazon's own definition** → full Series pages, read-through, ads to book #5 convert on the whole catalog
- Mangaka-style line: same pen name + publisher brand = catalog synergy: cozy café → bookshop → bakery; French bulldog → Corgi → Dachshund; Zodiac 12-book run + anthology
- Cross-sell inside every book (last page: "Love this? Get Volume 2")
- Format stacking per winner: paperback → (ebook at $2.99+ for the widened 70% band) → **audiobook via Virtual Voice** (free, no ads needed, low competition) → ACX crafted audio pay-for-production (exclusive = 40% royalty vs 25%)
- **Translations of proven winners**: Sean's 2 of his top-10 bestsellers are translated; ~$300 per 30K-word English→Spanish (human); raw Google-Translate = ban risk

### Portfolio scaling economics
- 1-in-3 to 1-in-8 titles become real winners — reinvest winners' profits into 3–5-book series arcs
- Go forward positioning vs AI spam (Self Publishing Empire's 2026 lesson): **medium/high-content products with higher creation barriers; use AI as editing/retouching assistant only**; niche-stacked quality titles
- Build catalog September–November so Q4 (Dec $15K months) lands on a full shelf
- Outsource point covers or full pages once a title proves itself; keep the pen name brand consistent for series read-through

## 8. Policy, IP & Account Health

### Absolutely banned (account killers)
- **"Amazon", Amazon trademarks, or Amazon-affiliation claims** in title/description/cover
- **Fake/paid/incentivized reviews** in any form (incl. QR codes for reviews, review-swaps, brokers)
- **Copyright/trademark infringement**: copyrighted characters, celebrity portraits, brand logos (anime/cartoon/Disney = banned; public domain pre-1930 US works OK per Jan 2026 cutoff rules with required differentiation: translation, original annotation, or ≥10 original illustrations)
- Copyrighted content tricks that "just change colors" — public domain content requires real added value
- Pixabay/Unsplash CC0 = commercial OK, but cannot resell standalone/unaltered, cannot use trademarked logos in compositions

### AI disclosure (mandatory since Sep 2023, enforced with indemnity)
Answer "AI-generated = Yes" at publish for any AI-generated text/image/translation (covers included). "AI-assisted" (heavily human-edited or human-prompted-original work) does not need disclosure. Misrepresentation → takedown / closure.

### 2026 KDP change log (verified)
- **70% ebook royalty band widened to $2.99–$12.99** (was $2.99–$9.99)
- **Pre-orders up to 18 months** (Kindle only), postponable twice by 6 months
- File downloads restored (manuscript + cover from Bookshelf)
- June 2025: paperback price bifurcation $9.99 = 60%/50% threshold
- Transparency barcode codes retired ~Nov 2025
- Identity verification (Gov ID) rollouts — keep legal name true in tax setup
- No official daily low-content submission cap verified in 2024–2026 help pages (community rumors of "3/day" unconfirmed); the real bottleneck is QC

### Account health checklist
- One identity + one pen name per high-quality series; a Publisher brand for the catalog
- Mislabeled low-content/no-content checkboxes = rejections keep A+ eligibility honest
- 7 keyword slots used honestly but densely (no title stuffing, no other brands)
- No medical claims ("anxiety cure", "therapy", "relieves stress forever" = removes; use "relaxation" framing instead)
- Never put "gift card in exchange for review" ANYWHERE (books, email funnels, TikTok outreach)

## 9. The 30-Day Launch Plan

### Week 1 — Research
- [ ] Install Book Bolt/BookBeam; identify 10 micro-niche candidates using the 5-point/7-point framework
- [ ] Validate: 200–2,000 results, ≥3 of top-10 BSR<100K, 2–3 books 50+ reviews, price carry $9.99+
- [ ] Read every 1-star review of incumbents → build the spec checklist
- [ ] Choose 1–2 niches (seasonal calendar awareness: if Sep–Oct → Halloween/Q4 catalog; else → evergreen)

### Week 2 — Build book #1
- [ ] Create 50–70 single-sided pages (B&W line art); clean bold 2–3pt lines; difficulty progression inside book
- [ ] Title = keyword literal; subtitle = audience+benefit+bonus; draft all 7 keyword slots
- [ ] Cover: finished-color sample demo; title readable at thumbnail; run A/B on personal taste
- [ ] Export interior (bleed-correct) + cover PDF from KDP cover calculator template

### Week 3 — Publish
- [ ] Upload; declare AI where true; B&W, no Expanded Distribution (low-content rules)
- [ ] A+ content 5–7 modules
- [ ] Create Pinterest boards + 20 pins; shoot flip-through Short; cross-post
- [ ] Set up free PDF magnet + MailerLite sequence
- [ ] Launch ads: $5/day auto, 3-week end date

### Week 4 — Learn/iterate
- [ ] Harvest search terms → manual exact campaign with negatives
- [ ] Gate: royalty ≥$3 → scale budget 20%/week if ACoS <25%
- [ ] Start book #2 in same series/pen name; repeat forever
- [ ] Reviews: Booksprout/BookBounty ARCs (never paid), target 10–20 reviews/month
- [ ] When a title proves BSR <20K steady → upscale it (translation, audiobook, more books in series, ads budget)
