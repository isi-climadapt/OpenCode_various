Somebody shook Banjo's tree!

Banjo the sugar glider is about to do his biggest glide ever when BOOM! His whole tree shakes. A big white light flashes across the bush. And then it starts to rain. Somebody did this, and Banjo is going to find out who. Was it Wombat, whose tummy is rumbling? Cockatoo, flashing her big white wings? Possum, thumping on the bark? Or the mysterious bird in the dark who can say BOOM all by himself?

Olive the koala knows the answer. She also knows a trick that makes thunder a little less scary.

Who Shook My Tree? is a funny thunderstorm read-aloud set in the Australian bush, with silly suspects, a surprise twist and a big snore on the very last page. Along the way, kids learn what lightning and thunder are, and how to count from the flash to the boom to tell when a storm is moving away.

Inside this picture book:

• A laugh-out-loud storm mystery with real Australian animals: a sugar glider, a koala, a wombat, a cockatoo, a possum, a lyrebird and singing green tree frogs.
• A gentle, brave-making story for kids who are scared of thunder, with a counting trick they can use in the next storm.
• Short, punchy dialogue that's fun to read aloud, with big BOOMs and FLASHes kids love to shout along with.
• True Facts from the Bush: two pages of real facts about lightning, thunder, storm safety, lyrebirds, frogs and the smell of rain.
• Soft, glowing stormy-night illustrations on every page.
• A cozy bedtime story and a thoughtful gift for kids ages 3 to 5, perfect for weather units at home, in preschool and in kindergarten.

Book 3 in the Banjo and Olive series. Each story stands on its own.
