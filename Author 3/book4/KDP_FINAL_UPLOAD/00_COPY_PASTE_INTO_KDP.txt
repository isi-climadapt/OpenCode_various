Somebody took Clover's pouch!

Clover the kangaroo joey has the best thing in the whole wide bush: her mom's pouch. It's her bed, her ride and her snack bar, and it's ALL hers. Then one morning she dives in and BONK! The pouch is shut tight. Somebody took it, and Clover is going to find out who. Was it Wombat, who has a pouch of her own? Pelican, with a BIG pouch under his beak? Possum, hiding a wriggly bump on her back? Or Echidna, who has a brand-new pouch?

Hugo the quokka smiles. Hugo always smiles. He already knows the answer.

Who Took My Pouch? is a funny read-aloud mystery set in the Australian bush, with silly suspects, a sweet surprise in the pouch and a cheeky last word from the newest member of the family. It's a gentle way to talk about a new baby, the worry of feeling too big, and the pride of becoming a big sister or big brother.

Inside this picture book:

• A laugh-out-loud pouch mystery with real Australian animals: a kangaroo joey, a quokka, a wombat, a pelican, a possum and an echidna.
• A warm new baby story for kids who are about to become a big sister or big brother.
• Short, punchy lines that are fun to read aloud, with big BONKs and GOTCHAs kids love to shout along with.
• True Facts from the Bush: two pages of real facts about joeys, pouches, wombats, pelicans, possums, echidnas and quokkas.
• Sunny, soft illustrations of the Australian grassland on every page.
• A thoughtful gift for a new big sibling ages 3 to 5, and a cozy bedtime story at home, in preschool and in kindergarten.

Book 1 in the Clover and Hugo series. From Hattie Moss, author of Who Peeled My Tree?
