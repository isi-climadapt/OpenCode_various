Description (Copy and paste directly into the KDP text box):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Somebody is peeling Banjo's tree!

Banjo the sugar glider loves his big, smooth gum tree. So when long strips of bark start curling off it, he knows just what to do: find the tree peeler! Was it Cockatoo, who chews everything? Possum, with that suspiciously soft bed? Echidna, scratching at the roots? Or Wombat, who says wombats don't climb trees?

Olive the koala knows the answer. If only she could stay awake.

Who Peeled My Tree? is a funny read-aloud mystery set in the Australian bush, with big expressions, silly suspects and a twist on the very last page. It's also a gentle story about change: sometimes things that look broken are really just growing.

Inside this picture book:

• A laugh-out-loud mystery with a cast of real Australian animals: a sugar glider, a koala, a cockatoo, a possum, an echidna, a wombat and a kookaburra.
• Short, punchy dialogue that's fun to read aloud, with big shouted words kids love to join in on.
• A gentle message about change and growing up, for kids facing something new.
• True Facts from the Bush: two pages of real facts about gum trees, sugar gliders, koalas and more (yes, wombats really do make cube-shaped poop).
• Soft, glowing twilight illustrations on every page.
• A great bedtime story and a thoughtful gift for kids ages 3 to 6.

Start with Banjo and Olive, and find out who really peeled that tree.
