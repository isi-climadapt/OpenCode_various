# Age band investigation (Author 3, Book 1)

> Checked 18 Oct 2026 on Amazon.com (Books department). Decision: **Ages 3-5**, used in every
> customer-facing field. Replaces the earlier "3-6", which matched no search phrase and mixed with
> a "4-8" keyword.

## 1. What shoppers type (Amazon search autocomplete, Books department)

Autocomplete lists completions in order of how often people search them, so it's a demand proxy
(it gives ranking, not volumes).

| Prefix typed | Age bands Amazon suggests |
|---|---|
| "story books for kids ages" | **3-5** (only suggestion) |
| "childrens books ages" | 0-3, **3-5**, 8-10, 1-3, 6-8, 8-12, "**3-5** best sellers", "4-8 best sellers my backpack" |
| "kids books ages 3" / "ages 5" | activity / **story** / coloring books for kids ages **3-5** |
| "kids books ages 4" | 4-6, then 4-8 (mostly coloring, dot-to-dot and maze books) |
| "books for 3 year olds" / "5 year olds" | books for **3-5** year olds |
| "toddler books ages" | 1-3, 2-4, **3-5** |
| "picture books for" | picture books for children ages 6-8 |

"3-6" never appears for any prefix. For story books, "3-5" is the band shoppers use; "4-8"
shows up mainly on coloring and activity books.

## 2. What the bestsellers' buyers report (Amazon.com Children's Books top 100)

Amazon now shows a customer-derived reading age ("from customers") on most listings. Story
picture books (24 to 48 pages, not board books) in the top 100:

| Book | Reading age shown |
|---|---|
| The Leaf Thief (the book we model) | 2-5 |
| The Gruffalo | 2-5 |
| Room on the Broom | 2-5 |
| Who Pooped on Me? | 3-5 |
| How to Catch a Monster | 3-5 |
| Creepy Carrots! | 3-6 |
| Pumpkin Jack | 3-6 |
| The Little Ghost Who Was a Quilt | 3+ |

Buyers of bestselling story picture books cluster at 2-5 and 3-5, younger than the trade label
"4-8" that publishers often print.

## 3. How the age reaches the listing

- KDP's Reading Age (minimum / maximum) feeds Amazon's age filters (Baby-2, 3-5, 6-8, 9-12).
  3 to 5 sits squarely in the "Ages 3-5" filter.
- Customer data later overrides the shown age, so the age must also be credible for the book:
  523 words, short dialogue, read-aloud. That fits 3-5 (and reads up to kindergarten).

## Decision

**Ages 3-5** everywhere: subtitle, keywords, KDP Reading Age (3 to 5), cover badge, description,
A+ panels, bio. One band only, no mixed bands across fields. "Kindergarten" stays in one keyword
to catch the older end without stating a second band.
