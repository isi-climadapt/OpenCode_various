## Who she is

UK picture-book and middle-grade author (Hertfordshire). Former librarian. 50+ books, 155 results under her name on Amazon.com books. Two lines matter:

- **The Leaf Thief series** (picture books, illustrated by Nicola Slater). This is the squirrel line and the reason she sells. US publisher Sourcebooks Jabberwocky, UK publisher Scholastic. New York Times bestseller.
- **Dark Unicorns** (Scholastic, chapter books, 5 books, 164 to 296 reviews each). Not relevant to us.

Everything else in her catalogue has a small footprint on .com. The squirrel series carries the brand.

Observations:

- **Sales are seasonal.** Mid-October puts the fall original at #23 overall and the Halloween title at #196. The summer book sits at #1,923 in October. Each title earns in its own season and the original earns every fall.
- **The original does the heavy lifting.** 4,217 reviews against 163 to 1,143 for the sequels. Sequels ride the first book's reach.
- **Retitle to fit the formula.** "Don't Touch That Flower" became "The Flower Thief" in 2025. The publisher moved the whole series onto one title pattern because the pattern sells.
- **Cadence:** one picture book roughly every 12 months from 2021 to 2024, then two a year from 2025 (plus three board books).

## Story engine (the approach we copy)

From the product descriptions and the eight interior spreads on the listing:

1. **The hero loves something in nature and counts or guards it.** A dramatic, possessive, slightly self-important animal.
2. **A natural change happens** (leaves fall, snow melts, days shorten, flowers bloom). The hero misreads it as a crime.
3. **The hero investigates with a calm friend.** The friend (Bird) is the straight man who already understands what's going on. The comedy is the gap between the hero's panic and the friend's patience.
4. **A line-up of suspects.** Each forest animal is accused, and each has a funny innocent explanation. This repeating pattern gives the book its rhythm and its page turns.
5. **The reveal is nature itself** (the wind, the season). The hero learns to live with change. Emotional theme on the listing: change, and the worry change can cause.
6. **Closing gag** on the last spread, so the book ends on a laugh.
7. **Back matter:** a non-fiction page explaining the science (why leaves change colour, Halloween traditions). Reviewers call this out as a reason to buy.

Text style: dialogue-led, short lines, 10 to 40 words a page. Big shouted display words mid-page ("Wait a MINUTE!", "MISSING!"), refrains and repeated counting, speech bubbles for the friend. Text is placed inside the art, often curving with the scene, rather than in a fixed text block.

## Art style

- Textured digital painting with a hand-made grain (crayon and gouache look), bright saturated palette led by the season (oranges and reds for fall, teal and navy for night).
- Expressive cartoon animals with big round eyes, rubbery poses, exaggerated tails. The squirrel is orange-red and reads clearly at thumbnail size.
- Page rhythm alternates **full-bleed spreads** with **white-ground vignettes** (small spot illustrations and a sequence of mini scenes on one page). White ground leaves room for text.
- Coloured speech bubbles (pink, yellow) and hand-lettered type in two or three sizes.
- Cover: hero character large and centred, holding the story object, title hand-lettered in the art, season-coloured border of leaves.

## Title formula

"The [seasonal object] Thief" + a search-stuffed subtitle: "(The Perfect Fall Book for Children and Toddlers)" / "The Perfect Spring Book for Children and Toddlers". The subtitle is a keyword line (season + "book for children and toddlers"). The series name is the first title.

## Reviews (what buyers value)

4.8 stars on 4,217 ratings, 89% five-star, 0% one or two star. Amazon's review summary and the aspect tags it shows (count of mentions in brackets): Content (251), Story (120), Illustrations (100), Entertaining (83), Humor (55), Fall-appropriate (38), Educational (22), Value for money (16).

Buyers say: children ask for it every night, the illustrations are beautiful, it explains seasonal change, the science facts at the end are useful, and the squirrel is funny. Individual review texts need sign-in, so this study uses Amazon's summary and tags only.

## KDP constraints that change the plan

Checked live on the KDP help pages, 17 Oct 2026.

- **Hardcover is not an option for a 32-page book.** KDP hardcover needs 75 to 550 pages. Her lead format (hardcover) is out. Author 3 publishes **paperback + Kindle**.
- **Premium colour ink is required.** Standard colour needs 72+ pages. Premium colour paperback allows 24 to 828 pages.
- **Print cost (Amazon.com), premium colour, 24 to 40 pages:** $3.60 regular trim, **$4.20 large trim** (anything wider than 6.12 in or taller than 9 in, so every picture-book size).
- **Royalty maths at 60%:** $9.99 list earns $1.79; $11.99 earns $2.99; $12.99 earns $3.59. Her paperbacks sell at $9.92 to $15.08, so $11.99 to $12.99 sits inside her range.
- **Trim options closest to hers:** 8.5 x 8.5 in (square, the common KDP picture-book size) or 8 x 10 in (portrait). Both are large trim, same print cost. Bleed 0.125 in; outside safe margin 0.375 in with bleed; gutter 0.375 in for 24 to 150 pages.
- **Kindle:** fixed-layout picture book (Kindle Kids' Book Creator), 70% royalty band $2.99 to $9.99. Her Kindle prices are $4.49 to $7.99.
- **Board books:** KDP doesn't print board books. That spin-off line is out.

## Copy plan (locked 17 Oct 2026, user approved)

What Author 3 copies:

- **Format:** 32-page full-colour picture book, ages 3 to 6. KDP paperback, premium colour, 8.5 x 8.5 in, plus a fixed-layout Kindle edition.
- **Story engine:** a natural change the hero misreads as a crime or disaster, an investigation, a line-up of suspects with funny innocent explanations, a reveal that nature did it, the hero accepting change, a closing gag.
- **Cast structure:** a dramatic, possessive hero plus a calm friend who already knows the answer, plus 3 to 5 suspect animals from the same habitat.
- **Text style:** dialogue-led, 10 to 40 words a page, shouted display words, a refrain, text placed inside the art.
- **Page rhythm:** full-bleed spreads alternating with white-ground vignette pages.
- **Back matter:** one non-fiction page with real facts about the natural change and the animals.
- **Series model:** one book per season or holiday hook, same cast, same title pattern, sequels lean on book 1.
- **Listing pattern:** keyword subtitle naming the season or hook and "book for kids", reading age declared.

What Author 3 doesn't copy:

- Her text, lines, refrains or jokes.
- Squirrel, Bird or any of her suspect animals and their gags.
- The "The [X] Thief" title pattern or the word "Thief" as the series hook. Author 3 gets its own title formula.
- Nicola Slater's look (orange squirrel, her palette, her lettering, her cover layout). Author 3 gets its own art style.
- Hardcover and board books (KDP can't print either at this page count).

## Market note for an Australian animal version

Her sales follow US seasons (fall leaves, Halloween, snow). An Australian animal raises a timing problem: Australian seasons run opposite to US ones, and the obvious Aussie nature changes (eucalypts shed bark not leaves, wattle blooms in late winter) don't line up with US buying seasons. Two ways round it, to decide in step 3:

- Use changes that happen everywhere and don't depend on hemisphere: day and night, the moon, rain and puddles, wind, shadows, tides, a molt or shed.
- Or keep seasonal hooks US buyers shop for (fall, Halloween, Christmas, Easter) and tell them from the Australian animal's point of view, which can itself be the comedy (an Aussie animal confused by snow, or by Christmas in summer).
