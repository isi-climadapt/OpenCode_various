Somebody frosted Banjo's tree!

Banjo the sugar glider has never seen snow. So when he wakes up and finds his gum tree covered in white, like a giant cake, he knows just what to do: find the tree froster! Was it Cockatoo, who is suspiciously white all over? Possum, whose face is covered in the stuff? Or Wombat, pushing a giant white ball? And why has Echidna disappeared?

Olive the koala knows the answer. If only she could stay awake.

Who Frosted My Tree? is a funny winter read-aloud set in the Australian bush, with silly suspects, a missing friend and a snowy surprise on the very last page. Along the way, kids discover what snow is and why some animals sleep right through the winter.

Inside this picture book:

• A laugh-out-loud snow-day mystery with real Australian animals: a sugar glider, a koala, a cockatoo, a possum, a wombat and an echidna.
• A gentle introduction to hibernation, perfect for winter units at home, in preschool and in kindergarten.
• Short, punchy dialogue that's fun to read aloud, with big shouted words kids love to join in on.
• True Facts from the Bush: two pages of real facts about snow in Australia, snowflakes and animals that hibernate.
• Soft, glowing winter illustrations on every page.
• A cozy bedtime story and a thoughtful winter gift for kids ages 3 to 5.

Book 2 in the Banjo and Olive series. Each story stands on its own.
