Snowy Cuties is a winter coloring book for kids ages 3-8, with 50 big, bold and cozy designs of penguins, polar bears, arctic foxes, reindeer and other snowy animal friends, and a fun fact on every page.

Slide down a snowy hill with a giggling penguin, build a snowman with a polar bear, and curl up with an arctic fox wrapped in its fluffy tail. Then get ready for the holidays: reindeer trim the tree, a polar bear hangs the stockings, and two huskies pull a sleigh full of presents. Every design has thick outlines and big open spaces, so little artists can finish each page and feel proud of it.

Inside this cozy winter coloring book:

• 50 Original Designs: Penguins, polar bears, arctic foxes, snowy owls, seals, moose, sea otters and more, plus 10 holiday scenes.
• Big & Bold Pages: Extra-thick outlines and large open spaces, great for crayons, markers and little hands.
• Fun Facts on Every Page: Learn real winter animal secrets as you color, like why penguins slide on their bellies and how a lynx walks on snow.
• Single-Sided Pages: Every design has a blank back, so markers don't bleed through and finished pictures are ready to display.
• Bonus Pages: A Color Test Palette, a Snowy Friends Checklist to check off all 50 designs, and a Snowy Artist Award.
• Large 8.5 x 11 inch Format: Crisp, high-resolution prints.
• A Great Gift: For Christmas stockings, winter break, snow days and cozy afternoons with hot cocoa.

Grab your mittens and your favorite colors. The Snowy Cuties are ready to play!
