Description (Copy and paste directly into the KDP text box):
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Yummy Cuties is a sweet treats coloring book for kids ages 4-8, with 50 big, bold and cozy designs of cute animals with cupcakes, ice cream, pancakes, pizza, fruit and picnics, and a real food fact on every page.

Frost a giant cupcake with a bunny baker, share a tall stack of pancakes with a happy piglet, and cool off with a penguin and a triple-scoop ice cream cone. Then toss pizza dough with a puppy chef, roll sushi with a kitten, hug a pineapple with a sleepy sloth and toast marshmallows by the campfire. Every design has thick outlines and big open spaces, so young artists can finish each page and feel proud of it.

Inside this cute food coloring book:

• 50 Original Designs: Cupcakes, donuts, ice cream, pancakes, waffles, cookies, pizza, sushi, fruit, veggies and more, each shared with a cute animal friend.
• Big & Bold Pages: Extra-thick outlines and large open spaces, great for crayons, markers and colored pencils.
• Fun Facts on Every Page: Learn real food secrets as you color, like why popcorn pops and why bananas are berries.
• Single-Sided Pages: Every design has a blank back, so markers don't bleed through and finished pictures are ready to display.
• Bonus Pages: A Color Test Palette, a Yummy Cuties Checklist to check off all 50 designs, and a Yummy Artist Award.
• Large 8.5 x 11 inch Format: Crisp, high-resolution prints.
• A Great Gift: For birthdays, Christmas stockings, rainy days and every little foodie.

Grab your favorite colors. The Yummy Cuties are ready to share a treat!
