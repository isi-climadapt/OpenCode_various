## RANKED SHORTLIST (5 concepts, demand-evidence ranked)

### 1. Aussie Cuties at Christmas — AU-specific Christmas, bold & easy + fun facts ⭐ RECOMMENDED
- **Window:** Q4 2026 GO, but **upload by ~Oct 12** (KDP live in 3 business days best case, up to 10 business days if routed low-content; AU detail page +5 days). Hard fallback Oct 19 = thinner ad ramp to Black Friday (~Nov 27).
- **Niche size:** "australian christmas colouring book" = **346 results vs 50,000+ generic Christmas** on .au — localization is the moat-dodge.
- **Demand proxies:** Bluey: Merry Christmas = **780 reviews** at A$4 on .au; UK-localized *British Christmas Colouring Book* (FairyWren) = **52 reviews on .au** — proves "localized Christmas" converts. Bold-easy Christmas format proven: *Christmas Bold and Easy* (Blue Jewel) = 57 rev, 4.8★; Coco Wyo *Cozy Countdown* Advent bundle = **#1 new release in both kids nodes on .com** within weeks (50 rev, 4.7★).
- **Competitive state:** 8 AU-Christmas colouring entrants published Jul–Oct 2026, **all 0 reviews** — supply is arriving (incl. foreign: German-edition Aussie Chrissy) but nobody owns it. Our 108pp/fun-fact/spec-transparency playbook wins on listing hygiene alone.
- **Series fit:** Book 2 of Gumleaf Aussie Friends; ~80% pipeline reuse (fonts, generators, layout, cover formula).
- **Risk:** zero-review supply spike means launch speed + ads decide the winner this season.

### 2. Great Barrier Reef — Ocean Friends (evergreen)
- **Demand:** ocean theme proven on .com (Paint by Sticker *Under the Sea* 8,498 rev; *Mermaids & Magic* 2,742; *Ocean Animals* sticker 1,050) and .au (Golden Age 291; FairyWren Under the Sea 94).
- **Gap:** GBR-specific kids titles exist with ~1 review each — "AU angle barely contested"; .com captured nodes had **zero outback/AU ocean entries**.
- **Timing:** no deadline pressure → best targeted at school-summer-holidays (~Nov 10 publish) or back-to-school (~Dec 22). Strong Book 3 candidate.

### 3. Cozy Capybara & Friends (kids-native)
- **Trend:** rising — Coco Wyo *Cozy Capybara* #7 new release on .com (69 rev, 4.8★), 62 rev / 4.9★ on .au; zero kids-native capybara styles in captured charts.
- **Risk:** theme already flooded with dozens of 2026 entries at 0–5 reviews; capybara-Christmas and capybara-Halloween variants already live. Late-wave entry needs a strong differentiator (fun-fact captions would be one).

### 4. Outback Australian Adventures
- **Evidence:** only **379 results** on .au (max 14 reviews) — zero moats, distinct visual identity (campfires, starry desert nights, road trains, dingoes), tourist-gift angle.
- **Risk:** low demand proof; supply arriving ahead of demand ("Outback Cozy Friends", "G'day Little Mate" at 0 reviews). Better as series Book 4 than Book 2.

### 5. Cozy Christmas Countdown (generic advent mechanic)
- **Evidence:** countdown mechanic proven by Coco Wyo's #1-NR Advent bundle; generic segment though is moat-heavy (Bluey 780, Coco Wyo Cozy Christmas 1,989 on .com, Advent Calendar 294 on .au). Only viable as a sub-angle of concept #1 (advent-style checklist page inside an Aussie Christmas book).

**Rejected:** dinosaurs (evergreen but highest moats: 4.9K/3.4K/3.1K reviews), space (incumbent-heavy 2.4K–2.6K), axolotl (peaked + flooded, higher entry moats than capybara).
