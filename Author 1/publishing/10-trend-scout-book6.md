## Age band (rule 16): **Ages 4-8**

- **(a) Search demand** (Amazon autocomplete, Books, amazon.com, today): "coloring books for kids ages" returns **4-8** first, then 8-12, 3-5, "4-8 girls" and 4-6. Theme prefixes ("sweet treats / food / kawaii / dessert coloring book for kids ages") return no band of their own, so the format-level band applies. 3-8 and 3-6 don't appear.
- **(b) Buyer evidence** (reading age on the listing): Kawaii Sweet Treats with Cute Animals 4-10; Kawaii Super Sweet Treats names "Ages 4-8" in its title; Silly Bear Unicorn 4-6 (customers); Caticorn 5-8 (customers); Notebook Doodles Sweets 6+ (customers). The adult-and-kids leaders show 7+ and 10+, which is the adult crossover this book doesn't target.
- **(c) Fit**: bold, easy pages a 4-year-old can finish, plus a one-line fact a parent reads aloud or an early reader reads alone. Same format as Pawsome Cuties.
- **Locked: Ages 4-8** in the subtitle, keywords, KDP Reading Age (4 to 8), cover band, description, A+ panels, alt text and bio.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **SWEET TREATS: cute animals with cupcakes, ice cream, pancakes, pizza and picnics** ⭐ RECOMMENDED. Bunnies baking cupcakes, a bear with a honey pancake stack, a penguin at the ice cream cart, a cat sharing sushi, a puppy pizza party, a hedgehog picking strawberries. Each page names a real food fact (bananas are berries, honey keeps for thousands of years, popcorn pops because of water inside each kernel, strawberries wear about 200 seeds on the outside). Why: it is the hottest live signal in the category (Cozy Cafe BSR 979, Food Drink & Sweets 4,365, Sweet Cutie 4,847), the kids' end of the lane is thin (321 reviews and below) and nobody adds facts. Cute animals carry Author 1's Cuties brand, and food is a new subject with no overlap with either author. Evergreen, so it lands for birthday gifts and Christmas stocking stuffers (live in early November) and sells all year. Risks: "Sweet Cutie" is a Coco Wyo title, so the name needs its own word; animal cast must avoid repeating Pawsome Cuties' pets as the main characters; labelled packaging prints words (book5 finding), so prompts keep to unlabelled food.
2. **CAPYBARA AND FRIENDS**. Strong trend (Cozy Capybara BSR 1,829) and a kid favourite. Risks: about 15 capybara titles launched in 2025 to 2026, and 50 true facts about one animal means repeats; a "friends" cast turns it into a generic animal book.
3. **EASTER / SPRING BABY ANIMALS** (bunnies, chicks, lambs, ducklings). Real basket-stuffer demand, but nothing to measure until February and the upload window is late January. Worth keeping for Book 7.
4. **FARM ANIMALS**. Clean theme for both authors, but the buyer data says toddlers (ages 1-3 and 2-7), which doesn't match Ages 4-8 and the facts.
5. Unicorns and magical creatures. Biggest raw demand, but incumbents at 4K to 22K reviews and no true facts. Pass.
