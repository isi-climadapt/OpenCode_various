## Age band (rule 16): **Ages 4-8**

- **(a) Search demand** (Amazon autocomplete, Books, amazon.com): "coloring books for kids ages" returns **4-8** first, then 8-12, then 3-5, then "4-8 girls". "dinosaur coloring book" adds "for kids ages 4-8"; "unicorn coloring book for kids ages 4-8" also appears. 3-8 and 3-6 never appear.
- **(b) Buyer evidence** (Reading age on the listing, mostly "from customers"): Bug Book Did You Know? 3-6, Dinosaur Did You Know? 3-6, Silly Bear Dinosaur 3-5, Silly Bear Unicorn 4-6, Silly Bear Mermaid 4-6, Cute Cats Ages 4-8 4-7, Cute Dogs Ages 4-8 4-8, Paint by Sticker Pets 5-8, Capybara 50 Fun Facts 4-12, Big Book of Colouring Animals 3+. The kids' bestsellers in this format sit inside 3 to 8, centred on 4 to 6, and the pet titles that name a band all use "Ages 4-8".
- **(c) Fit**: big bold pages a 4-year-old can finish, plus a one-line fun fact a parent reads aloud or an early reader reads alone. 4-8 fits; 3-5 undersells the facts.
- **Locked: Ages 4-8** in the subtitle, keywords, KDP Reading Age (4 to 8), cover band, description, A+ panels, alt text and bio. No "3-8" anywhere.
- Found in passing: Snowy Cuties (book 4) mixes bands: "FOR KIDS AGES 3-8" on the cover band and "ages 3-8" in the description, "ages 4-8" in the keywords.

## Ranked shortlist

1. **PETS: puppies, kittens and other pets ("Pet Cuties" working name)** ⭐ RECOMMENDED. Cute puppies and kittens plus bunnies, hamsters, guinea pigs, goldfish, a parrot, a turtle, a pony, doing cozy everyday things: bath time, walks, naps, birthday parties, vet check-ups, playing fetch. Why: pets are the only animal theme besides dinosaurs with a title in the Children's Coloring Books top 30 (#11); cats and dogs each carry 500 to 2K-review books, but nobody has a bold-easy mixed-pets kids book with a fact on every page; true, kid-friendly facts are plentiful (cats' whiskers measure gaps, dogs' noses print like fingerprints); no overlap with Author 1's four books; evergreen, so it sells into Christmas gifting (live early November) and all year after. Fits the Cuties line and the Coco Wyo direction (single-theme cute animals). Risk: two new "pets cuties" titles in October, so the title needs a collision check in Stage 2.
2. **DINOSAURS ("Dino" working name)**. Biggest demand of any animal theme and reaches boys strongly. Risks: old incumbents with 1K to 5K reviews, at least four cute-dino launches since July 2026 (one already called *Dino Cuties*), and a softer fit with Author 1's cozy, real-animal brand.
3. **EASTER / SPRING BABY ANIMALS (bunnies, chicks, lambs)**. Real basket-stuffer demand in the US, but the window is March 2027. Better as Book 6, uploaded late January.
4. **BUGS AND GARDEN FRIENDS**. Facts angle proven by *Bug Book* (1.2K). Spring-leaning; lower ceiling than pets.
5. Valentine's. Thin demand and a two-week window. Pass.
