## 2. Long bio (~250 words — Amazon Author Central, publisher page)

Matilda Hayes writes and illustrates cheerful colouring books from a sunroom studio
in Melbourne, Australia — usually with a cup of weak tea, two kids testing every
page, and a kelpie named Banjo snoring on her feet.

Her idea started the way many good ones do: on a rainy Sunday. Colouring was the
family's favourite wind-down, but the bold-and-easy books they kept buying were thin,
grey and over by Tuesday. Matilda wanted pages with big open shapes that invited the
crayons in, characters cute enough to fall in love with, and a little true fact to
make kids giggle and learn. So she started drawing them herself.

The result is **Gumleaf Kids Press** and the *Gumleaf Aussie Friends* series — chunky,
kawaii-style Aussie animals in cozy, happy scenes: sleeping koalas, potion-brewing
kangaroo witches, reef friends and Halloween cuties. Every page follows her house
rules: thick, confident outlines; generous white space; nothing scary; a fun fact on
every single page; and enough room on the backs for marker mess and fridge-display
masterpieces.

Matilda believes there are no mistakes in art — purple koalas allowed, rainbow
wombats encouraged — and that twenty minutes of colouring can reset a whole day.
When she's not drawing, she's walking Banjo, refereeing a backyard cricket match, or
planning the series' next big adventure.

Made with love in Australia.

## 3. Medium bio (~110 words — A+ about-the-author module, media kit)

Matilda Hayes is an Australian author and illustrator from Melbourne who creates
cheerful, bold-and-easy colouring books designed to spark imagination and creativity.
Her series, *Gumleaf Aussie Friends*, stars kawaii-cute Australian animals in cozy
scenes — koalas, kangaroos, wombats, quokkas, reef friends and Halloween cuties — all
drawn with extra-thick outlines, big open spaces, and a fun fact on every page. She
works in a sunroom studio with two cheeky kids as her test audience and a sleepy
Australian kelpie named Banjo as chief nap officer. Matilda believes there are no
mistakes in art and that twenty minutes of colouring can reset a whole day. She lives
under wide sunny skies, and every book is made with love in Australia.
