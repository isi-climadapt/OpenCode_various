--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Pawsome Cuties is a pet coloring book for kids ages 4-8, with 50 big, bold and cozy designs of puppies, kittens, bunnies, hamsters and other pet friends, and a fun fact on every page.

Splash in a bubble bath with a happy puppy, chase a ball of yarn with a playful kitten, and hop for joy with a bunny doing a binky. Then meet a hamster with stuffed cheeks, a guinea pig popcorning, a smiling axolotl and a pony napping on its feet. Every design has thick outlines and big open spaces, so young artists can finish each page and feel proud of it.

Inside this cute pet coloring book:

• 50 Original Designs: Puppies, kittens, bunnies, hamsters, guinea pigs, parrots, turtles, ponies, a hedgehog, an axolotl and more.
• Big & Bold Pages: Extra-thick outlines and large open spaces, great for crayons, markers and colored pencils.
• Fun Facts on Every Page: Learn real pet secrets as you color, like why a bunny does a binky and how many baby teeth a puppy has.
• Single-Sided Pages: Every design has a blank back, so markers don't bleed through and finished pictures are ready to display.
• Bonus Pages: A Color Test Palette, a Pet Friends Checklist to check off all 50 designs, and a Pawsome Artist Award.
• Large 8.5 x 11 inch Format: Crisp, high-resolution prints.
• A Great Gift: For birthdays, Christmas stockings, rainy days and every young pet lover.

Grab your favorite colors. The Pawsome Cuties are ready to play!
