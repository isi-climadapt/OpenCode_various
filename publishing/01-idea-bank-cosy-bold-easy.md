## Part 2 — Format ideas (what goes between the covers)

1. **Pure bold & easy** — 1 giant design per page, blank backs. The Coco Wyo formula. Fastest to make safest.
2. **Colouring + fun-fact captions** — one-line animal/scene fact under each image. Justifies higher AUD, parents love the ed-value angle. Pair with Aussie animals (B1/B3).
3. **Colouring + micro-activities** — every 4–5 designs add: trace & colour, count & colour, find 5 things, finish-the-pattern. Activity hybrids hold the HIGHEST prices on .au (activity book observed at A$27.49 with 493 reviews).
4. **Progressive difficulty** — book opens ultra-bold simple, gets gradually a touch more detailed. Keeps toddlers 3–6 AND 8–12 in one purchase. Include a "level stars" corner icon per page.
5. **Story-day structure** — scenes follow a character through one day (Capybara wakes up → breakfast → beach → sunset). Differentiator; binge-able; perfect for series ("Capy's Week", "Capy's Christmas").
6. **Frame-able art** — dashed "keep & display" border on designs + a结束时 certificate/gallery page. Parents love display-worthy output; earned reviews mention it.
7. **Two-in-one editions** — same art Christmas edition + birthday edition (re-use art base = 2× catalogue speed).

## Part 3 — Page-count & build specs (all B&W, 8.5×11, amazon.com.au)

**AU print cost quirk = major advantage:**
- B&W large-trim interior, 24–108 pages = **A$5.28 FLAT** (a 108-page book prints for the same cost as a 24-page book!)
- Therefore: **never publish a thin book. Fill to 108 pages — free real estate.**
- >130 pages the per-page formula (A$2.42 + A$0.022/pg) gets more expensive; sweet spot ≤108.

**Standard build (108 pages total):**
- Front matter: 1 title, 1 copyright/"this book belongs to", 1 colour-tips page = 3pp
- **50 bold designs**, each with its own blank back page = 100pp
- Bonus back matter: 1 "My Art Gallery", 1 achievement certificate, 3 frame-able keepsake designs = 5pp
- = 108 pages, **50 paintings + extras**
- Unit economics: list A$16.99 → 0.60 × 16.99 − 5.28 = **A$4.91 profit/sale** (A$14.99 → A$3.71; A$19.99 → A$6.71)

**Toddler build (thick-line, ages 1–3, 108pp):** 40 oversized designs + gallery/certificate; list A$14.99 → A$3.71.

**Mega-activity build (Christmas/travel, 4–8, 108pp):** 38 designs + 16 activity pages + gallery; list A$19.99–24.99 → A$6.71–9.71.

**Kidult premium build (8–12, story-day format):** 45 designs + fact captions + certificate; list A$16.99 (+ matte cover) → A$4.91.

Page-count rule of thumb vs comps: most kids bold-easy competitors list 40–100 single-sided pages in the description — 50 designs lands at the top of perceived value.
